Agency Assessment

Understand your current situation.

These twelve folders maintain person-level records about your time, health, language, background, identification, finances, legal matters, conflicts, education, employment, regulation, and community.

twelve folders

Each folder has a different job

Each folder establishes a different record and gives the agent a different job. The folders do not run in a required sequence.

01
TimeSTARTING
DefinitionThe measured period in which something takes place.
In Universe CityYour agent establishes the commitments that already use your time and the time you want to protect. Work, commuting, sleep, meals, preparation, appointments, responsibilities, and rest can all be placed on the calendar as you confirm them.
What you buildA calendar your agent maintains as your schedule changes.
02
Health
DefinitionThe condition of a person's body and mind, and the care taken to maintain it.
In Universe CityAppointments, medications, symptoms and mood, recorded as they happen. What you are due for, what you have let go, and who you last saw about it.
What you buildA health record holding your appointments, your medications, what you have reported, and what is currently due.
03
Language
DefinitionThe system of words a person uses to understand others and to be understood.
In Universe CityReading, writing, speaking, grammar and punctuation, placed at a level and tracked. Where your comprehension currently limits what you can do.
What you buildA placement across reading, writing and speaking, and a route to raising it.
04
Background
DefinitionA person's origins, family and history.
In Universe CityWhere you come from. Parents, grandparents, the people you are related to, close friends, and the timeline of your own life. Not jobs and not addresses.
What you buildA family tree and a personal history timeline, and a record of who you can actually call.
05
Identification
DefinitionProof that a person is who they say they are.
In Universe CityWhich documents you hold, what each one is for, when it expires, and how it is replaced. Universe City records that a document exists and its status. It never records the number on it.
What you buildA document registry listing what you hold, what has expired, what is missing, and how to obtain it.
06
Finance
DefinitionThe management of money.
In Universe CityWhat comes in, what goes out, what you owe, and what is left. Budgeting, saving, credit and financial literacy. This is education, not advice.
What you buildA money picture showing income, obligations, and what remains after both.
07
Legal
DefinitionRelating to the law and to matters the law decides.
In Universe CityOpen legal matters, court dates, charges and deadlines. Where to go for help and how the process works. Universe City is not a law firm and does not advise on the merits of a matter.
What you buildA matters file holding every open issue, its deadline, and who handles it.
08
Mediation
DefinitionSettling a difference between two sides.
In Universe CityConflicts with a landlord, an employer, a coworker, a family member. What happened, what was said, what was asked for, and what was agreed. Universe City is not a therapist and does not provide counseling.
What you buildA conflict record holding what happened, what was said, and what was agreed.
09
Education
DefinitionGaining knowledge or skill through study or instruction.
In Universe CityWhat you have already learned and can prove, and a running list of what you want to learn next. Your agent sources the actual next step rather than leaving the interest to fade.
What you buildA credentials log, and a list of what you want to learn with a costed route to each.
10
Employment
DefinitionWork done for payment.
In Universe CityEvery job you have held, when, for whom, and who can confirm it. The record you need when a form asks for five years of history.
What you buildA verified work history you can evidence.
11
Regulation
DefinitionKeeping something running the way you want it to run.
In Universe CityWhat you do for your own sake, with no obligation attached. Hobbies, going out, rest, personal interests you want to explore, and the small comforts that make a day bearable.
What you buildA record of what actually restores you and what only fills the hours.
12
Community
DefinitionThe people in a place and the relationships between them.
In Universe CityWhere you live, who you know there, what you show up for, and what you contribute. Volunteering, civic participation, and making a connection on purpose.
What you buildA community log holding your area, the people in it, and what you turn up for.
