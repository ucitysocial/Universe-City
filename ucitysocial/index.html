how intake works

Each folder starts differently

Your agent collects the information each folder needs instead of running the same questionnaire four times.

01

Time reconstructs a real day

Your agent asks you to walk through a day you actually lived. The times you confirm become the first version of your calendar.

02

Inventory simulates a move-in

Imagine moving into a furnished apartment with no groceries or household supplies. For each item you name, your agent asks how many you normally buy and how long that amount usually lasts.

03

Salary reviews the last full month

Your agent records the income, bills, and regular expenses from a month that already happened. An amount can stay estimated until you confirm a better number.

04

Standards uses guided discovery

Your agent asks which routines and rules you return to when you want to function consistently. A standard is added only after you confirm the rule, its intention, the value behind it, and the impact you expect.

why this exists

Why I built
Universe City

I lost my mom suddenly in 2016. I was 20, heading into 21, and I lost my guiding light.

She was the person I could call, fall back on, move back in with, love and hug. She had known me from the beginning of time. Losing her meant realizing I would now be responsible for understanding myself as fully as she had.

I had to learn how to manage my time, money, goals, priorities and standards while creating a direction of my own. I knew I could not be the only person who felt overwhelmed by the ordinary work of adulthood.

Universe City grew from years of asking what makes life easier to manage, what people should understand before adulthood, and how those responsibilities can be organized into something usable.
