the problem
You were given advice.
You were not given a system.

The information already exists.

It is scattered across websites, offices, apps, family, workplaces, and whatever you have worked out for yourself.

Nobody is short on advice. What is missing is the part where it gets organized, applied, kept current, and connected to everything else. That work has always been yours to do alone, for free.

the architecture

Four departments

  • Life is already complex. Universe City divides those complexities into 4 core areas called Departments.
  • Each Department holds 12 folders. A folder is one part of your life, worked for one month.
  • Every folder runs the same way: track the behavior, observe the patterns, adjust through systems, and maintain through daily, weekly or monthly management practices.
  • Nothing resets at the end of the month. The folder stays open, and both you and your agent keep working from it.

why this exists

A blank university,
where life is the teacher

My mom was my first agent. She held the parts of my life I could not hold yet and handed them over as I got ready for them.

She died in 2016. Losing her is how I found out how much of my life she had been running with me.

Most people lose that at some point, and plenty never had it. Universe City is my attempt at replacing the structure, not the person. It is built deliberately without emotion, because that is not what it is designed for.

Therapy is great for that. Case management is great for rock bottom. This is meant to be somewhere in between.

SYSTEM

One department at a time

Curriculum and software with no person attached to it. The system is your agent: you message it, it keeps your record, and it answers from what you have given it. Take one department or take all four.

This tier pays a person. A real agent at Universe City who knows your file and picks up where the system stops. One agent carries up to forty-eight clients.

Taken out of every membership before anything else is paid. It is a separate nonprofit with separate books, explained below.

TWO RULES THAT DO NOT MOVE
  • 01Nobody we refer you to pays us. Universe City keeps a network of local businesses, clinics, attorneys, mechanics, landlords, and services that clients need. None of them pay to be in it. They are listed on what clients reported after being sent there. No fee, no commission, ever.
  • 02The books are published every quarter. Revenue, what each tier brings in, what the trust holds, what it funded, and what the founder is paid. In full, four times a year.

the trust

Twelve percent of every membership goes to a nonprofit

THE COMPANY

Universe City

A social enterprise, structured as an LLC. It runs the folders, the software, the memberships, and the agents. It is a for-profit company and it says so.

THE NONPROFIT · EST. MARCH 2023

Universe City Community Development Corporation

A 501(c)(3) community development corporation. A CDC is a nonprofit that exists to develop one specific place and the people living in it. CDCs build and hold housing, run community facilities, make grants, and fund local services. They are governed by residents of the area they serve rather than by outside donors.

What the trust can be used for

Funds are held by city. Money contributed by clients in Denver is spent in Denver, on what Denver clients vote for. Every vote and every disbursement is published, and the CDC keeps separate books so it can be audited apart from the company.

Grants to clientsWritten directly to a client who needs one. A deposit, a car repair, a certification, a replacement document, a month of rent.
Third spacesRooms with a scanner, a printer, a kitchen, laundry, a quiet room, and a mailing address that does not change. Leased first, owned later.
Housing and schoolsThe long end of what a CDC does. Property acquired, held, and built for the people whose memberships funded it.
