# Instructions for every tool that writes in this repository

Read [LANGUAGE_STANDARD.md](LANGUAGE_STANDARD.md) before you write or edit text a resident can read. It governs every word. Where another file disagrees with it, the Language Standard governs.

Before a commit, run:

```
python3 scripts/language-check.py
```

The total on October 10, 2026 was 238. A commit shall not raise the total.

## The rules that are broken most often

1. The forty eight units are folders. A system is the working product of one folder. Write forty eight folders.
2. The person is a resident in all text the person can read. The person is a client in agent documents and in the agent console. Member is retired.
3. The arrangement is a residency. Membership is retired.
4. The signed in area is the Resident Portal. The action is Log in. Dashboard and Sign in are retired.
5. Public text states no price.
6. These words are prohibited where they stand in for a class you have not named: anything, something, things, everything, someone, somewhere, various, several, certain, as needed. Name the exact class.
7. A statement built on a contrast is prohibited. The five constructions are listed in clause 6.11.2. Write one positive statement of the fact.
8. A list is permitted only when it is complete. A sample of cases is prohibited.
9. Text shall not supply a scene from one kind of life. State what the folder contains and give the question it answers.
10. Each sentence shall be true for a seventh grader, a person without housing, and a person who has never managed their own affairs.
11. Metaphor and every other figure of speech are prohibited. Each sentence is literally true as written.
12. Resident facing text contains no hyphen and no dash.
13. A button that performs an action contains a verb and a specific object. Continue, Get started, and Next are prohibited.
14. Text signed by the founder in the first person is text the founder wrote or approved. Do not write new sentences in her name.
